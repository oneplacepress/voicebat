Three Pillars of VoiceBat

🦇 Both Audio & Text (BAT)

Our revolutionary approach gives users the flexibility to choose how they consume messages. Hear the emotion, tone, and personality in someone's voice, or read the transcribed text when audio isn't practical. Messages include both formats simultaneously, preserving the human element while maintaining text convenience.

  • ✅ Emotional context preserved
  • ✅ Flexible consumption options
  • ✅ Human personality in messages
  • ✅ Automatic transcription included

🎙️ Push-To-Talk Safety

Eliminate distractions by employing Push-To-Talk – users hold down a mic button to talk without having to look at a screen. Perfect for hands-busy situations.

📡 LoRa Mesh Independence

Remove dependence on wireless providers by transmitting BAT messages via inexpensive LoRa mesh networks which users can set-up themselves.

How VoiceBat Works

1️⃣ Record Voice

Hold the push-to-talk button and speak your message naturally. Up to 30 seconds of high-quality audio is captured with your voice, emotion, and personality intact.

2️⃣ Auto-Transcribe

Advanced AI automatically converts your voice to text, creating a readable version while preserving the original audio. Recipients get both formats in one message.

3️⃣ LoRa Transmission

Messages travel up to 2 miles through LoRa mesh networks, reaching recipients even without cellular or WiFi coverage. Independent, reliable communication.
